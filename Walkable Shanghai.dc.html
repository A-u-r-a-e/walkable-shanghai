Reasoning

What I assumed

From the deck: the unit of value is the route, and the failure the app fixes is a walk cut short. So every route carries a dead-ends count and names the walker who published it — that is what makes word-of-mouth routes trustworthy at scale.

Tracking is supporting, not the point: it exists to close the loop from walking a route to posting it to the feed.

Language

The app runs in one language at a time, switched under Settings on the profile screen. Both strings live in the layout, so nothing shifts or reflows when a reader changes it — Chinese sets on a looser leading, which the prose blocks account for.

The system

Classical, held to the letter: paper ground, hairline rules instead of cards-on-fills, gold as stroke only, Cormorant for figures and titles over Lora for prose, tabular numerals for figures. Selected controls take a gold inset ring on solid paper — no tinted fills anywhere.

The map is real OpenStreetMap geometry graded to the paper ground: the Wukang Road loop, Suzhou Creek east bank, Fuxing Park, the Bund and Century Park.
